# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: June 24, 2026*

## Overview

CombineIQ ("we", "our", "the app") is a football combine tracking app for coaches. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, including minor athletes whose data may be entered by coaches.

## Data We Collect

CombineIQ stores the following data locally on your device and in your iCloud account:

- **Coach profile:** Name and email address provided via Sign in with Apple
- **Program data:** Program name, team names, and program settings
- **Player data:** Player names, positions, and combine performance results entered by coaches
- **Subscription status:** Purchase history managed by Apple and RevenueCat

## How Data Is Stored

All program and player data is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) under the coach's personal iCloud account. We do not operate our own servers or databases. Your data remains under your control through your Apple ID.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not track your location
- We do not use advertising identifiers
- We do not sell or share personal data with third parties
- We do not use cookies or web tracking
- We do not collect device fingerprints

## Third-Party Services

- **Apple iCloud (CloudKit):** Data sync and storage — governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **RevenueCat:** Subscription and purchase management — governed by [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **TelemetryDeck:** Privacy-preserving aggregate analytics (no personal data collected) — governed by [TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy)

## Children's Privacy (COPPA)

CombineIQ is designed for use by coaches (adults). Coaches may enter performance data for minor athletes. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

- The app does not collect personal information directly from children
- Player data is entered and managed exclusively by authorized coaches
- No behavioral tracking or advertising profiles are created for any user
- Parent/guardian access is provided through a separate, read-only parent portal with coach-controlled invite codes

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored in your iCloud account for as long as you use the app. You can delete your account at any time from Settings > Delete Account, which removes your coach profile. Program data shared with other coaches remains accessible to those coaches. To delete all program data, the program admin can remove it before deleting their account.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at [support@combineiq.app](mailto:support@combineiq.app).
