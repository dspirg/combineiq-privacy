# CombineIQ Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** April 2, 2026

**Effective Date:** April 2, 2026

---

## Overview

CombineIQ is a football combine tracking application designed for adult coaches to manage, track, and analyze player combine performance. The app is operated exclusively by adult coaches -- children and minor athletes do not directly interact with the app or create accounts.

This Privacy Policy describes how CombineIQ ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

---

## Information We Collect

### Apple User ID (via Sign in with Apple)

When you sign in to CombineIQ, we receive an opaque Apple User ID from Apple's Sign in with Apple service. This identifier is used to:
- Authenticate your account
- Sync your data across your devices via Apple CloudKit
- Associate your subscription and purchase history

We do **not** request or store your email address, name, or other personal details from Apple's sign-in service.

### Purchase History (via RevenueCat)

When you purchase a subscription or player credit pack, the transaction is processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, our in-app purchase management partner. Data collected includes:
- Subscription status (active, expired, in grace period)
- Consumable credit pack purchase records
- Transaction identifiers

This data is linked to your Apple ID and used solely for managing your subscription and credit balance within the app.

### Aggregate Usage Analytics (via TelemetryDeck)

We collect anonymous, aggregate usage data through TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used and improve the experience. This includes:
- Which features are used (e.g., creating a combine session, viewing rankings)
- Session frequency and retention metrics

TelemetryDeck is a privacy-preserving analytics service that:
- Does **not** collect personal information
- Does **not** perform device fingerprinting
- Does **not** create behavioral profiles
- Does **not** use advertising identifiers
- Is hosted in the European Union
- Does **not** require an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt

### Player Performance Data (via Apple CloudKit)

Coaches enter player information and combine results into the app. This data includes:
- Player names (first and last)
- Position (e.g., QB, WR, RB)
- Height and weight measurements
- Graduation year
- Combine event results (e.g., 40-yard dash time, vertical jump height)

This data is stored in the coach's personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit service. CombineIQ's developers do **not** have access to individual player records -- Apple manages the storage infrastructure, and data remains under the coach's iCloud account.

---

## Information We Do NOT Collect

CombineIQ does **not** collect:
- Email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses
- Location data or GPS coordinates
- Photos, videos, or media files
- Behavioral tracking or advertising identifiers
- Device fingerprints
- Browsing or search history
- Health data (beyond athletic performance metrics entered by coaches)
- Social media profiles or contacts
- Financial information (payments are handled entirely by Apple)

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

CombineIQ is designed for use by adult coaches, not children. **We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).**

### How our app handles minor athlete data:

1. **Coach-operated model:** All data is entered by adult coaches on behalf of players. Children do not create accounts, sign in, or interact with the app directly.

2. **Parental consent attestation:** Before creating a program that includes players under 13, coaches are required to attest that they have obtained verifiable parental consent for each minor athlete whose data they enter.

3. **Limited data scope:** Player profiles contain only athletic performance data (name, position, physical measurements, and combine results). No contact information, photos, or location data is collected for any player.

4. **No child-directed features:** The app does not include features designed to appeal to children, such as games, social networking, or content creation tools.

5. **No third-party child data sharing:** None of our third-party service providers (RevenueCat, TelemetryDeck) receive personal information about minor athletes. RevenueCat processes only adult coach purchase transactions. TelemetryDeck collects only anonymous, aggregate usage data.

If you believe that a child under 13 has directly provided personal information to us without appropriate parental consent, please contact us immediately at **danspirgen@gmail.com** so we can take appropriate action.

## Data Storage and Security

### Where your data is stored

| Data Type | Storage Location | Managed By |
|-----------|-----------------|------------|
| Player performance data | Coach's iCloud account | Apple CloudKit |
| Purchase and subscription data | RevenueCat servers | RevenueCat |
| Aggregate usage analytics | EU-hosted servers | TelemetryDeck |
| Authentication credentials | Apple's identity service | Apple |

### Security measures

- **CloudKit:** Data is encrypted at rest and in transit by Apple. Access is controlled by the coach's iCloud account credentials.
- **RevenueCat:** SOC 2 Type II compliant. Data transmitted over TLS.
- **TelemetryDeck:** No personal data stored. EU-hosted with GDPR-compliant infrastructure.
- **Local device:** Core Data stores are protected by iOS device encryption and app sandboxing.

---

## Data Retention

- **Player performance data:** Persists in the coach's iCloud account until the coach deletes it from within the app or deletes their iCloud account. We do not independently retain copies of this data.

- **Purchase and subscription records:** Retained by RevenueCat in accordance with their data retention policy and applicable tax/financial regulations. See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) for details.

- **Usage analytics:** TelemetryDeck retains aggregate analytics data on a 24-month rolling window. No individual user data is retained. See [TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/) for details.

- **Authentication data:** Apple User IDs are retained as long as the coach's account exists in the app. Coaches can delete their account and all associated data from within the app.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access your data:** All player and program data is visible within the app at all times.
- **Delete your data:** Coaches can delete individual players, teams, programs, or their entire account from within the app. Deleted data is removed from CloudKit sync.
- **Request data deletion:** Contact us at **danspirgen@gmail.com** to request deletion of any data associated with your account.
- **Opt out of analytics:** TelemetryDeck does not collect personal data, but you can contact us if you wish to discuss analytics data collection.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), you may also have rights under the GDPR including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
